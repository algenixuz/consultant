# Privacy Policy — Maslaxatchi

**Last updated:** 8 October 2026

**Application:** Maslaxatchi (`uz.algenix.consultant`)

**Published by:** Algenix (algenix.uz)

**Contact:** algenixitcompany@gmail.com

## Who this app is for

Maslaxatchi is an internal work tool for employees of a retail store. It is not a
consumer app. Accounts are issued by the employer; nobody can register from
inside the app. An employee uses it to look up products, build a basket, look up
or register a customer, and read and confirm a sales contract.

Each store runs its own server. The app connects only to the server address the
employer configures on the device, and all data entered in the app is stored on
that server, under the employer's control. Algenix supplies the software; the
employer is the controller of the data in it.

## What the app handles

**Employee account.** Phone number and password, used to sign in. A session
token and the last used phone number are kept on the device so the employee does
not have to type them again.

**Customer records.** When an employee registers or opens a customer, the app
handles the customer's full name, date of birth, gender, PINFL (national
identification number), passport or ID document details, address, phone number,
and the photograph held in the national identity record.

**Identity verification.** Confirming a customer's identity and their agreement
to a contract is done through MyID, Uzbekistan's state-backed identification
service. The customer's face is captured by the MyID component and checked
against the national identity record by MyID. The app does not store the face
image or any other biometric template, and the verification is carried out
inside MyID, not by this app. MyID processes this data as an independent
service under its own terms.

**Contracts and payments.** The app displays contract documents, payment
schedules and invoices, and records that a contract was confirmed. These are
data records only. The app does not take payments, hold card details, or connect
to any payment provider.

**Camera.** The camera is used for two things: reading QR codes (the server
address, and a contract's code), and the MyID face check. Nothing from the
camera is stored by the app or sent anywhere other than to MyID for the
verification described above.

## What the app does not do

- It does not sell or rent personal data.
- It does not share customer data with any other person or company for their own
  purposes. Data goes to the employer's own server, and to MyID where identity
  verification requires it.
- It contains no advertising, no advertising identifier, and no third-party
  analytics or tracking.
- It does not read contacts, messages, call logs, photos, files, or location.
- It is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Internet, network state | Communicating with the employer's server and MyID |
| Camera | Reading QR codes and the MyID face check |
| Vibration | Feedback when a QR code is recognised |

## Storage on the device

The app keeps the sign-in session, the chosen server address, the chosen
language and theme, and the current basket on the device itself. All of it is
removed when the employee signs out or the app is uninstalled.

## How long data is kept, and how to have it removed

Customer records, contracts and payment records live on the employer's server
and are kept for as long as the employer's own retention rules and the
applicable law require. Requests to see, correct or delete that data are handled
by the store that holds it; the store can be reached through its own contact
channels, or write to algenixitcompany@gmail.com and the request will be passed on.

Data processed by MyID during identity verification is governed by MyID's own
privacy terms.

## Security

Traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS. The session token stored on
the device is removed on sign-out and whenever the server reports it has
expired. Access inside the app is limited to the employee account signed in.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published at the same address,
with a new date at the top. Significant changes will also be communicated to the
employers using the app.
